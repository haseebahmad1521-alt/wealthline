Privacy Policy.

Effective November 3, 2026.

Wealthline is an app that ranks the world's largest fortunes and lets you follow the people behind them. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app at haseebahmad1521-alt.github.io/wealthline/app, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

What we collect.

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), and the first name you choose to add.
  • Google sign-in: if you continue with Google, we receive your name, email address, Google account identifier and profile picture link. We use them only to create and sign in to your account. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.
  • Device verification: a random device identifier kept in your browser, the list of devices you have verified, and the one-time codes we email you when you sign in on a new device.
  • What you save: your watchlist and saved views. These are private to your account.
  • AI chat (when available on your plan): your messages, the replies, short notes the chat remembers about you, your usage and any moderation warnings.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers keep standard service logs (such as IP address, time and request) to run and secure the service.
  • On your device: the app stores your session, preferences (such as display currency) and an offline copy of the app in your browser's storage.

Why we use it.

To sign you in and keep your account secure, to save your watchlist and settings, to provide AI chat replies, to enforce usage limits and our Terms, and to answer you when you contact us. We do not use your data for advertising.

Who processes it.

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web app.
  • Google provides "Continue with Google" when you choose it.
  • xAI generates AI chat replies; your chat messages and relevant context are sent to it for that purpose.
  • Loading public content contacts the sources it comes from, such as Wikimedia Commons (portraits) and the Frankfurter exchange-rate service; they receive your IP address as with any web request.

Google user data.

Wealthline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google sign-in data only to provide sign-in, never transfer it except as needed to run the service or by law, and never use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data.

We keep your account data while your account exists. To delete your account and the data tied to it, or to get a copy of it, email us from your account's address; we act within 30 days. Service logs are kept by our providers for a limited period. You can clear the data stored on your device at any time in your browser's settings.

People in the rankings.

Profiles describe public figures using published sources such as public rankings, filings and news, each with its source. If you are featured and want a correction, contact us.

Children.

Wealthline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes.

We will post any change here with a new effective date, and tell signed-in users in the app about significant changes.
