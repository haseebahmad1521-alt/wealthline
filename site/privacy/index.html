Wealthline

Privacy Policy.

Effective November 25, 2026.

Wealthline ranks the world's largest fortunes and lets you follow the people behind them, on the web and in the iPhone and Android apps. This policy explains what we collect, why, who handles it and what you can do about it. Wealthline is run by Arctic Imaginarium (“we”). We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

What we collect.

  • Account details. Your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider), and a first name if you choose to add one. The first name is used to greet you.
  • Google or Apple sign-in. If you continue with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address (with Apple this may be a private relay address), account identifier and, for Google, a profile picture link. We use them only to create your account and sign you in. We don't access your mail, contacts, files or anything else in those accounts.
  • Device verification. A random identifier for each device or browser, a record of the devices you've verified (trusted for 90 days), and the one-time codes we email you when you sign in on a new device.
  • What you save. Your watchlist and saved views. Only you can see them.
  • AI chat (on Pro and Premium plans). Your messages and the replies, short facts the chat remembers about you to keep conversations consistent (it is instructed never to remember health, religion, politics, sexuality or money), your daily usage, and any moderation warnings or pauses on your account.
  • Your plan. Which plan you have, where it came from and when it ends.
  • “What if” questions and Versus matches. Questions you type on the World screen are stored with a count of how often they're asked, and are public: they can appear in “Most asked this week” and be opened by link. They are not linked to your account, so don't include anything personal in them.
  • Profile interest. When a Pro or Premium member opens a profile, we count it for that profile so it gets fresh updates sooner. This count holds no information about you.
  • Technical data. Our hosting providers keep standard service logs (such as IP address, time and the request made) to run and secure the service.

On your device.

The app keeps your sign-in session, your device identifier and verification token (with the email address it belongs to), your display currency, your score in the World guessing game, a copy of public data so screens load quickly, and an offline copy of the web app. On iPhone and Android, the device identifier and token are kept in the system's secure storage. You can remove all of it by signing out and clearing the app's or site's data. We don't set cookies.

Why we use it.

To create your account, sign you in and keep it secure (performing our agreement with you); to save your watchlist and settings and answer your chats (the same); to enforce usage limits, prevent abuse and keep the service running (our legitimate interest in a safe, working service); and to answer you when you contact us. We don't use your data for advertising or profiling.

Who processes it.

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, sign-in emails and server functions.
  • xAI (Grok) writes AI chat replies and answers “What if” and Versus questions. For chat it receives the recent messages of that conversation, the facts the chat remembers about you and your first name if you set one, but not your email address or account identifier. We ask xAI not to store requests.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in when you choose them.
  • GitHub Pages and our web host serve the web app and this website.
  • Public sources your device contacts directly: Wikimedia Commons (portraits), the Frankfurter service (European Central Bank exchange rates) and Polymarket (prediction-market odds on the World screen). Like any web request, they receive your IP address, but nothing about your account.

Some of these providers process data outside the EEA. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Google user data.

Wealthline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google sign-in data only to provide sign-in, never transfer it except as needed to run the service or by law, and never use it for advertising.

How long we keep it.

Account data, your watchlist, saved views and chat history are kept while your account exists. You can delete a chat conversation (with what it remembered) at any time in the app, and remove watchlist entries and saved views yourself. Public “What if” questions are kept because they aren't tied to anyone. Providers keep service logs for a limited period.

Deleting your account.

Email haseebahmad1521@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We delete your account and everything tied to it (sign-in details, verified devices, watchlist, saved views, chats, remembered facts, usage and plan) within 30 days and confirm by email. Public “What if” questions stay, since they can't be traced to you. You can ask for a copy of your data the same way.

Your rights.

You can ask to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, object to how we use it, or restrict it. Contact us and we'll answer within 30 days. If you're in the EEA, you can also complain to your data protection authority; in Norway that is Datatilsynet.

People in the rankings.

Profiles describe public figures from published sources such as public rankings, company filings and news, each with its source. AI chats “as” these people are clearly marked simulations. If you're featured and want a correction, or object to something shown, contact us.

Children.

Wealthline isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes.

We'll post any change here with a new effective date, and tell signed-in users in the app about significant changes.
